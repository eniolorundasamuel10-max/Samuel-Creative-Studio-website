About Me

Design with a purpose, built around your business.

I’m Eniolorunda Samuel, a passionate Nigerian graphic designer and digital marketing specialist. I help businesses communicate their message through purposeful, attractive and professional designs, from a first logo to the visuals that carry a campaign.
